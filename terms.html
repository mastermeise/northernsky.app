The subscription

The price for each is shown in the app before you buy, in your local currency; the monthly plan is about two dollars a month and the yearly plan about ten dollars a year, though the exact figure is whatever the App Store shows you.

  • The free week. The monthly plan starts with a free week for anyone who has not had one before. Nothing is charged until the week ends. The app schedules a reminder a day before that, so the first charge is never a surprise. The yearly plan starts today, with no trial.
